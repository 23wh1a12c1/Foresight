4.Accountability and Consequences
To ensure a respectful, productive, and inclusive environment during our group project, 
please adhere to the following guidelines. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary 
actions, including legal measures or permanent dismissal from the internship program.

Mutual Respect : Treat all colleagues and group members with respect, dignity, and 
kindness. Disparage no one based on their race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, or 
any other personal characteristics
Professional Behavior : Maintain a professional demeanor in all interactions. Avoid any 
forms of harassment, bullying, or any behavior that could be perceived as inappropriate.
Active Engagement : Be proactive in your participation. Contribute ideas, volunteer for 
tasks, and engage in discussions
Responsibility : Take responsibility for your portion of the project. Meet your deadlines, 
and produce quality work that reflects well on you and your team
Open Communication : Keep the lines of communication open. Update your group on 
your progress, ask for help when needed, and offer assistance to others
Conflict Resolution : Approach conflicts constructively. Aim for resolution through 
dialogue and compromise. If a resolution cannot be reached within the group, escalate 
the issue to a supervisor or mentor for guidance.
Understanding of Consequences : Be aware that failure to adhere to these guidelines 
not only affects the team's dynamics but may also lead to disciplinary actions. 
Depending on the severity of the misconduct, consequences can range from a formal 
warning to legal action or permanent removal from the internship
Reporting Issues : If you witness or experience misconduct, report it immediately to 
your supervisor or the designated contact person. All reports will be treated with 
confidentiality and addressed promptly.
 
By fostering a culture of respect, accountability, and collaboration, we can ensure a 
positive and productive internship experience for everyone involved. Let's work together to 
make our project a success.
