##Outlier Detection
#What is Outlier?
Outliers are the points that significantly deviate from the overall patterns of a dataset.

#Why it is important?
Improve Model Performance
Enhance Data Quality
Detect Fraud and Anomalies

#Common Outlier Detection Techniques:
1.Visual Method
Box Plot/Scatter Plot/Histogram

2. Statistical Methods:
1. Z-Score:(Standard score method)
Z=X-u/sigma
X=data point
u=mean of the dataset
sigma=standard deviation

Moreover the data points with |Z-score|>3 are considered as the outlier

#Removing the outliers
2.#IQR(Interquartile Range Method)
IQR=Q3-Q1
Q1-First quartile
Q3-Third quartile

Lower Bound=Q1-1.5*IQR
Upper Bound=Q3+1.5*IQR

Example:
Dataset=[5,7,8,12,14,18,22,24,27,30,35,40,100]
Q1(25th percentile)=12
Q3(75th percentile)=30

Calculate IQR=Q3-Q1=30-12=18

Calculate the Bounds
Lower Bound= Q1-1.5*IQR = 12-1.5*18=12-27= -15
Upper Bound= Q3+1.5*IQR = 30+1.5*18=30+27=57

Identify the outlier
Any value below lower bound -15 is an outlier- Not in this case
Any value above upper bound 57 is an outlier- Here 100 is an outlier
